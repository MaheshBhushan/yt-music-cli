Install. Sign in. Play.

Install pipx and Python 3.11 or newer first. mpv is the separate audio player; ytm can help you install it.

pipx install ytm
ytm install-mpv
ytm auth

GOOGLE SIGN-IN

You choose the access.

Run ytm auth on your computer. Google opens in your browser, asks you to choose an account, and shows the requested YouTube permission.

ytm uses access to display your library and carry out playlist and like actions you request. Credentials are saved on your computer; this website does not receive them.
